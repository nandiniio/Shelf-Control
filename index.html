The reader behind the shelves

A little about me

Thoughts, quiet corners, and the books that make life sweeter.

Hi there! Welcome to my reading nook. I built Shelf Control because I needed a cozy, gentle corner of the internet where keeping track of books feels just as magical as diving into chapter one.

I am one of those readers whose to-be-read list grows faster than any bookshelf can hold. I love morning coffee with a paperback, dog-eared bookmarks, scribbling small thoughts into page margins, and spending rainy afternoons lost in secondhand bookshops.

Reading has always been my favorite way to slow down time. Whether it's literary fiction that breaks my heart, cozy fantasies that feel like a warm blanket, or poetry discovered on a quiet Sunday, every book is a small world I'm grateful to visit.
